# ADR 0012: The EIN preinjector: represented from the cathode, tracked by an injector code

Status: accepted in part (2026-10-13; the gun as an element, 2026-10-14): its representation and the beam it delivers; the code that tracks it is not chosen yet. Scope: `eicvibe.importers.parmela`, `eicvibe.machines.preinjector`, the EIN machine (`eicvibe.machines.ein`), magnet fields in the lattice (amends ADR 0001), beams in the machine files (amends ADR 0005), what the lattice engines take (ADR 0006), and route runs (ADR 0007).

## Context

The EIN began at the LTR: its routes started from the LTR's initial optics, with a Gaussian beam matched to them. Before the LTR is the preinjector, about 77 m from the cathode: a 320 kV DC gun with a GaAs photocathode for polarized electrons; solenoids; a 16 degree gun dipole; two Wien filters (spin rotators); a 197 MHz buncher, a tapered 1.3 GHz capture cell and a 1.3 GHz booster to about 10 MeV; fourteen 3 m S-band (2856 MHz) travelling-wave structures to 750 MeV; quadrupole triplets, steerers and BPMs; and two diagnostic lines, one to a YAG screen and Faraday cup (the gun dipole reversed), one to a Mott polarimeter. Its designer's files are in the EIN folder's `einlattices/Preinjector`: the engineers' survey (`toengineer/Main_lattice.xlsx`, with names, positions and the requirement sheet; `Gun_diag.xlsx`, `Spin_diag.xlsx`), a PARMELA 3.4 deck from the cathode through the linac (`parmela/rr6_sep.inp`) and the beam it delivered to the LTR (`afterlinac_wk_jun13_1p7nC.dat`: 1.7 nC, 750 MeV, 20 000 particles; the LTR's initial optics are exactly this beam's), and a GPT deck of the gun region with 3D field maps and Wien filters (`gpt/au1.gpt`). The baseline is this deck's: 750 MeV and 1.7 nC per linac bunch (the BAR accumulates them), newer than the 400 MeV and about 7 nC of earlier EIC papers.

The lattice engines (Xsuite, TrackPad) cannot track it. The beam starts at rest at the cathode, which only integration in time through the gun's field covers; at 320 keV (beta 0.79) its speed changes inside the cavities, and the bunching works by those speed differences, which thin RF kicks get wrong; space charge, going as 1/(beta^2 gamma^3), is about 3000 times stronger at the gun than at 10 MeV and 10^9 times stronger than at 750 MeV, and the designer's codes keep it on throughout; the energy grows 2300 times along one line, where the engines' branches have one reference momentum; and the polarized beam's spin goes through the Wien filters. Facilities track such injectors with codes built for them (ASTRA, GPT, IMPACT-T, OPAL-T, PARMELA), in time and with space charge, and either hand the particles to an s-based code once the beam is relativistic (CLARA's ASTRA/GPT to Elegant at some 35 MeV) or keep the injector code to the end; online models run a fast part of the model, or a surrogate trained from scans (the LCLS online model).

## Decisions

1. **The preinjector is part of the EIN machine: three branches, from its survey and its deck** (`eicvibe.machines.preinjector`, `eicvibe.importers.parmela`). `prj` from the cathode to where the survey connects it to the LTR (76.765 m), forked into the LTR's start; `prj_gun`, the gun diagnostic line; `prj_spin`, the spin diagnostic line. The survey gives the elements, their names and positions; the deck, at the same positions to 0.1 mm, their strengths. The gun is an element of its own, PALS's `EGun` ("an electron gun ... starting from the cathode"), `prj-gun30`: the survey's first drift, from the cathode to 30.7 cm, where the deck tracks in the gun's field map; the cathode stays a marker at its entrance. The gun dipole is one magnet that both the main line (+16 degrees, towards +x) and the gun line (reversed) pass through, a shared component like the BAR septum (ADR 0001), with the main line's pole faces (4 degrees) for both; the 15 degree spin kicker (the deck's "reserved diagnostic bend", after the capture cell) is one too, off for the main line and a bend for the spin line. Each S-band structure is one travelling-wave cavity, its input coupler's field (the deck's "fringe" cell) included; bucking solenoids are their two coils, each on its own supply as the survey's requirements say. The floor places it by its fork into the LTR, whose position does not move; it agrees with the survey's coordinates to 1.3 mm at the LTR and 1 mm at the side lines' ends. `python -m eicvibe.machines.ein FOLDER` imports it with the rest; `add_ein_preinjector` adds it to a machine folder that has none and leaves every other file's content as it was.
2. **Its magnets are given by their fields, as designed** (amends ADR 0001). A normalized strength needs the reference momentum at its magnet, and along this line that is what tracking the beam finds, not a design value. So the lattice adopts PALS's field forms of `MagneticMultipoleP` (`BnN`, `BsN` per length, `BnNL`, `BsNL` integrated; T, T/m, ...) beside the normalized ones, one form per order; solenoids had `Bsol` already. Normalized strengths stay the rule; fields are for lines like this one. Supplies set what the lattice gives (`Bn1`, `Bsol`), with no rigidity in the conversion from current, also for the line's magnets at zero; solenoids get supplies. Quadrupoles take the deck's gradients with the electrons' sign (PARMELA's positive gradient focuses the tracked particle horizontally; its own comments give the QF gradients as negative fields). Cavities take the deck's frequency, its field amplitude times their length as voltage, and its phase (within plus or minus pi; PARMELA's zero, not interpreted). The gun takes `RFP.voltage`, the deck's scale of its field map (0.914 of the map's 350 kV, as the deck's comment gives it: 319.9 kV), and no frequency: it is DC. PALS's `EGun` is under construction and lists no voltage; Bmad's PALS writer gives an `e_gun` an `RFP` group, which is followed here (Bmad marks DC by a zero frequency, which PALS's positive `frequency` does not allow, so it is left unset). The gun changes no reference energy (`dE_ref` 0): the branch's reference particle is already the gun's exit energy. Steerers are at zero: the deck's values correct the alignment errors switched on in its run. The reference particle of all three branches is the gun's (320.37 keV, the deck's gun dipole energy): the bends at that energy take the reference field.
3. **The lattice engines leave it to an injector code.** Xsuite and TrackPad refuse a branch with an electron gun, whose beam starts at rest, or whose magnets are given as fields, for these reasons (`beyond_lattice_engines`); the machine's optics say "not for a lattice engine" instead of inventing them, and the viewer shows that. Which injector code tracks it is not decided: ASTRA (free, the common choice), GPT and PARMELA (the designer's; GPT licensed), IMPACT-T and OPAL-T (open, parallel), or Bmad (installed here, PALS-native, with an electron gun element, time Runge-Kutta in field maps, cathode space charge and spin, though its tracking in time is to be shown correct before it is relied on). It will be an engine of its own kind: run on demand when the preinjector's settings change, minutes per run, its result kept by settings; later perhaps a surrogate for live response.
4. **The beam it delivers is part of the machine's files** (amends ADR 0005). `beams/<branch>.yaml` and `.csv` hold a bunch at a branch's start: its particles (x, px, y, py, zeta, delta about the reference momentum given), charge and source. The EIN's `beams/ltr` is the deck's run at its interface to the LTR (PARMELA's columns: positions in m, momenta over mc, z ahead of the centre, total energy in MeV), about the LTR's reference: 53 and 32 nm emittances, beta 9.03 and 20.30 m, 0.913 mm long, 0.23 % momentum spread with its chirp, as PARMELA's interface gives. A route that starts at such a branch's start shoots it (ADR 0007): each shot is a sample of its particles, at its charge, moved by the shot-to-shot jitter, instead of a Gaussian matched to the initial optics. The routes keep starting at the LTR.
5. **Names are the survey's EIC names where it gives them, made valid where it does not.** The survey names elements `prj-<device><position in cm>`; they become the lattice names and, through `naming.yaml`'s `names`, the EIC names. Where the survey's would not be valid or unique: a bucking solenoid's coils are `prj-sol175:1` and `:2`; the Wien filters `prj-wf260`, `prj-wf355` and the YAG screen `prj-yag274` (the survey gives them no device letters); the quadrupole corrector `prj-qc355`; the gun line's own steerer after the dipole `prj-thv144:2`, its valve `prj-vlv284` and Faraday cup `prj-fc284`; the spin line's elements, unnamed in its survey, `prj-<device><main-line position>` (`prj-spk856`, `prj-mott1141`). Device abbreviations the naming repository does not list (`thv`, `wf`, `tbd`, `qc`, `spk`, `mott`, `yag`, `vlv`) are temporary, as the EIN's area prefixes are, until SEG assigns them. Screens, Faraday cups and polarimeters are instruments that are not BPMs (tags `SCREEN`, `FARADAY_CUP`, `POLARIMETER`).

## Not represented yet

- The gun's high-voltage supply as a device: its voltage is in the lattice but not yet a setting. The gun's vessel and anode: its length is the survey's drift, and its field map's extent is not known without the map.
- The field maps PARMELA and GPT use (the deck's Poisson and Superfish maps, `GUN350_22.T7`, `197M.T7`, `1300MT_SIG.T7`, `1300M.T7`, read by its `poisson` and `cfield` commands, are not in the folder: to ask the designer for).
- The Wien filters' crossed electric and magnetic fields (drifts tagged `WIEN_FILTER`; PALS's `ElectricMultipoleP` is not adopted) and the 2 cm quadrupole triplets that come with them in the GPT deck.
- The energy at the spin diagnostic line, which no design file gives: its bends take the gun's reference field (the capture cell before it runs near -90 degrees, bunching rather than accelerating; to confirm).
- The triplet after the survey's connection point, which the deck tracks into: the LTR's own design begins there.
- PARMELA's interface is 22.7 cm before the survey's connection point: the beam is the one the LTR's design starts from.

## Validation (2026-10-13)

- The main line is 76.76491 m, with the gun (0 to 0.307 m, 319.9 kV, DC), 16 solenoid coils, 27 quadrupoles, 17 cavities (3 standing-wave, 14 travelling-wave at 2856 MHz), 23 BPMs; every name is a valid, unique EIC name.
- The floor matches the survey to 1.3 mm at the LTR, the Faraday cup and the Mott polarimeter; the LTR does not move.
- Both engines refuse it, for its fields; the machine's optics and the viewer say so.
- The delivered beam reproduces PARMELA's interface to 1 %; a route from the LTR shoots samples of it at 1.7 nC.
- Added to a machine folder, it leaves every branch file as it was and only adds to the device and naming files.
